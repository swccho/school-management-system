# Modern School Website & Management System

## Software Project Overview

The **Modern School Website & Management System** is a comprehensive digital platform designed to modernize both the **public online presence** and the **internal academic management processes** of a school.

The platform combines a **modern public-facing website** with a **secure role-based management portal**, allowing schools to efficiently manage academic, administrative, and communication activities from a centralized system.

This system serves multiple user groups including:

* School administrators
* Teachers
* Students
* Parents / guardians
* Public visitors

The goal is to reduce manual administrative work, improve transparency, enhance communication, and create a professional digital presence for the school.

---

# Project Goals

The main goals of the system are:

* Create a **modern and professional school website**
* Digitize daily **school administrative operations**
* Reduce paperwork and manual record management
* Improve communication between school and guardians
* Provide **secure access to academic information**
* Publish notices, events, and results online
* Maintain centralized academic data
* Build a **scalable and extensible school platform**

---

# System Architecture Overview

The platform consists of **two major parts**.

## 1. Public School Website

The public website serves as the **official online presence of the school**.

This section is accessible without login and allows visitors to learn about the school and access public information.

### Key Features

* School introduction
* School history
* Mission and vision
* Principal's message
* Staff and teacher directory
* Notice board
* News and events
* Admission information
* Class routines
* Public result lookup
* Photo gallery
* Contact information
* Download center

This section focuses on **presentation, communication, and information publishing**.

---

## 2. School Management Portal

The management portal is a **secure internal system** used by authorized users.

It enables the school to manage:

* Academic structures
* Student records
* Teacher information
* Attendance
* Examinations
* Results
* Notices
* Admissions
* Reports
* User access and permissions

This portal acts as the **operational backbone of the school**.
